I built Nubis, a free and open-source Windows app for SSH, RDP and SFTP, to manage servers spread across several clouds and on-prem networks.

• Pulls your servers from AWS, Tencent Cloud, GCP, Azure and OCI, and imports on-prem lists (Excel/CSV, ~/.ssh/config, mRemoteNG, Ansible, Active Directory)
• Tabbed SSH/RDP/PowerShell, Ctrl+K to connect, and an SFTP panel that follows the terminal's current folder
• Built-in Claude Code panel that can run commands and manage hosts, with your approval and an audit log. It can't read your saved passwords or keys.
• Local only: no telemetry, no account, data encrypted with Windows DPAPI

GitHub: https://github.com/windevase/nubis (MIT, portable zip on Releases)
